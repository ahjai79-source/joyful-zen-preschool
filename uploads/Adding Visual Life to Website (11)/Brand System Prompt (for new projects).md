## Brand personality
Warm, joyful, calm, and human. It feels like a beautifully kept **notebook** — hand-drawn, encouraging, never corporate or clip-arty. Think a thoughtful teacher's handwriting in the margins next to clean, confident typography. Playful but premium. Aimed at parents, so it reads grown-up and trustworthy, with childlike warmth in the accents (not childish).

## Typography (Google Fonts)
- **Headlines / titles:** `Hanken Grotesk`, weight 800, tight letter-spacing (-0.025em to -0.03em), line-height ~1.05. Big and confident.
- **Body / UI:** `Mulish`, weights 400–700, line-height ~1.6 for readability.
- **Handwritten accents:** `Caveat`, weights 600–700 — used for margin notes, captions, eyebrows, and "teacher's pen" callouts. Often slightly rotated (-1.5° to -3°). Never use Caveat for body copy.
- Small uppercase labels: Hanken Grotesk 800, ~11–13px, letter-spacing .08–.12em, uppercase, muted gray.

## Signature visual treatments (this is what makes it "us")
1. **Yellow highlighter swipe** behind a key word: a `#FFE36B` block rotated ~-1.5°, sitting *behind* the text (like a real marker). Use to emphasize ONE word in a headline.
2. **Hand-drawn red circle** (`#EA4B3C`, ~2.8px irregular `border-radius:52% 48% 50% 52%`, rotated ~-5°) around a stat or word — e.g. our "6:1" ratio or "Max 8".
3. **Squiggly underline** under links/keywords: a colored `border-bottom` with `border-radius:50%` and a slight rotation, so it looks drawn by hand.
4. **Caveat margin notes** in a brand color, slightly rotated, pointing at things with little hand-drawn arrows (e.g. "↑ the whole point", "spots go fast!", "be kind ♥").
5. **Hand-drawn ink doodles** of school supplies — pencil, eraser, protractor, ruler, apple, open book, paintbrush, simple equations (2 × 8 = 16, ½ + ¼ = ¾), basic Spanish words (hola = hello). Style: **ink outline + flat brand-color fill**, loose and friendly, NOT thin generic line icons. Age-appropriate for 6–10 (addition, times tables, simple fractions).
6. **Tilted polaroids:** photos in a white frame, rotated a few degrees, with a Caveat caption underneath ("happy at school ♥").
7. **Rounded pills/badges:** `border-radius:99px` for tags like "Ages 2½–5", "Full-day", schedule chips.
8. **FOCUS · CREATE · GROW** motif — three words, each with a different treatment (underline / yellow highlight / green circle) separated by ✦ sparkles. This is a recurring signature.

## Voice & copy rules
- Warm, plain-language, encouraging. Talk to parents like a trusted teacher.
- **No em dashes anywhere** (brand rule). Use commas, periods, or rewrite. (En dashes for ranges like 9:00am–3:00pm are fine.)
- Sentence case for most things; reserve ALL CAPS for small labels.
- Light, tasteful use of ♥ and ✦ in Caveat accents only — never emoji elsewhere.
- Real specifics over filler. No invented stats or padding.
