✦ A day in the life

Our daily rhythm

9:00
Gentle Arrival

Soft music and warm hellos as we ease into the morning.

9:30
Creative Studio

Paint, playdough, and collage exploring color and texture.

10:00
Circle Time

Songs, stories, weather, and our daily question on the rug.

10:20
Nourish & Outdoor Play

A healthy snack, then running, climbing, and digging outside.

11:00
Mindful Movement

Gentle stretches, breathing, and balance games.

11:15
Spanish Enrichment

¡Hola! Songs, colors, and playful words in a second language.

11:30
Montessori Work Cycle

Self-directed discovery that grows focus and independence.

12:00
Community Lunch

Sharing a meal family-style, practicing kindness and conversation.

12:30
Wind-Down & Storytime

Cozy books and soft voices as we settle into the afternoon.

1:00
Rest & Recharge

Quiet time and naps for those who need them.

2:40
Snack & Reflection

One more snack and a gentle look back on our day.

3:00
Home Time

Hugs and happy goodbyes. Extended care runs until 5:30pm.

Extended hours available · 3:00–5:30pm
