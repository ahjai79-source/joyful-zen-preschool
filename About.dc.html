✦ Our story

A little school built on warmth.

Joyful Zen Preschool started with a simple belief: little kids learn best when they feel safe, seen, and free to play. Our teachers keep classes small so every child gets real attention, every single day.

✦ Meet the director

Hello, I'm Charlotte

I'm Charlotte Acevedo, the founder of Joyful Zen Preschool. Having my son Zen inspired me to create this magical space, where children feel safe to blossom, play, and grow. Being a parent taught me my best lessons about connecting with kids, and I've now spent over 6 years working with young children. Alongside our wonderful team of early childhood educators, we prepare your child for kindergarten and beyond, one joyful day at a time. I can't wait to meet your little one!

✦ What we believe

Our approach

Play-based

Kids learn best through hands-on play, not worksheets.

Montessori & Reggio inspired

Self-directed discovery that follows each child's curiosity.

Bilingual moments

Everyday Spanish songs and words woven into the day.

1 teacher : 4 kids

Small groups so every child is truly seen and known.

✦ Our teachers

A team that knows your child's name

Our teachers are warm, present, and genuinely love this age. With just four children per teacher, they get to know each family well, celebrate every milestone, and make school feel like a second home.
